Current Openings

Where are we?

The Lee Lab is based at the Indiana University School of Optometry in Bloomington. Bloomington is a lively college town with a strong research community and collaborative environment across neuroscience, vision science, and engineering.

Open positions

We are recruiting postdoctoral fellows, graduate students, and technicians (full-time or part-time).

What we look for

We welcome applicants with training in neuroscience, physiology, biology, or engineering. Experience with electrophysiology, imaging, or quantitative data analysis is a strong plus, but curiosity, rigor, and the ability to execute matter more than specific techniques.

How to apply

If you are interested in joining the lab, please email JL428 [at] IU.EDU with:

  • a brief statement of research interests
  • your CV
  • contact information for 2–4 references

Include a short note on why our research direction aligns with your goals.
